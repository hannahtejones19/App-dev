This is my favorite series "The crown" because it shows how love can be complicated when duty comes first.

The story follows Queen Elizabeth II as she suddenly becomes queen at a young age, forcing her to put her responsibilities above her personal life. Because of this, her relationship with Prince Philip is often tested, as love and duty begin to clash.
As the series goes on, it also explores other emotional relationships within the royal family, especially Princess Diana’s story, which shows how difficult it is to find real happiness under pressure and expectations.
In the end, the series highlights that even if there is love, not everything works out perfectly—especially when you have a role that demands so much from you.
